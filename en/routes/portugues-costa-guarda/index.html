Route description

Coastal Portuguese Way from A Guarda

The Coastal Portuguese Way from A Guarda allows pilgrims to travel along the entire southwestern coastline of Galicia while taking in breathtaking views over the Atlantic Ocean.

Starting from the picturesque fishing village of A Guarda and the iconic Monastery of Santa María de Oia, the route continues past historic Baiona, the city of Vigo, and the Redondela inlet.

From Redondela it joins the traditional Portuguese Way, passing monumental Pontevedra, the thermal spa town of Caldas de Reis, and the Jacobean cradle of Padrón to complete the distance required for the Compostela certificate.

Highlights

  • Castro de Santa Trega and the fishing harbour of A Guarda
  • Monastery of Santa María de Oia facing ocean breakers
  • Monterreal Fortress and Baiona marina
  • Panoramic views of the Cíes Islands and Vigo estuary
  • Old quarter and Basilica of Santa María in Pontevedra
  • Thermal mineral springs in Caldas de Reis
  • Plaza del Obradoiro and Cathedral of Santiago
