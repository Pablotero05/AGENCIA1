Route description

English Way from Ferrol

The English Way was the maritime route used in the Middle Ages by pilgrims arriving from Great Britain, Ireland, and Scandinavia at the northern ports of Ferrol and A Coruña.

Starting at the historic port of Ferrol, the route winds along spectacular coastal rías before venturing into the dense green forests and tranquil farmland of the Galician interior.

Covering over 100 km, it allows walkers to earn the Compostela in under a week, visiting medieval jewels like Pontedeume and Betanzos, and experiencing the untamed solitude of Hospital de Bruma.

Highlights

  • Departure from Curuxeiras port in Ferrol and coastal promenade
  • Medieval town of Pontedeume and historic stone bridge across the Eume river
  • Well-preserved medieval center and famous gastronomy in Betanzos
  • Passing through legendary Hospital de Bruma in serene nature
  • Lush native oak and chestnut forests
  • Exciting arrival at the Plaza del Obradoiro

Day by day planning

Recommended Stages

1

Ferrol → Neda

You start in Ferrol, departing from the port and following the estuary. A gentle stage, initially urban, gradually becoming rural. Perfect for getting into rhythm effortlessly.

15 km 3–4 h Difficulty Low
2

Neda → Pontedeume

Pleasant walk between small hamlets and green fields, keeping the estuary in sight for part of the way. Arrival in picturesque medieval Pontedeume.

16 km 4 h Difficulty Low
3

Pontedeume → Betanzos

First demanding stage. Right after departure there is a steep climb setting the tone for the day. Alternates woodland and country lanes. Finish in beautiful Betanzos.

20–21 km 5–6 h Difficulty Medium
4

Betanzos → Hospital de Bruma

The toughest stage of the route. Long, with elevation gain and secluded stretches. Very rural with few services, but intensely authentic. Requires good stamina.

24–28 km 6–7 h Difficulty High
5

Hospital de Bruma → Sigüeiro

More manageable stage following the previous day's effort. Forest paths, tranquility, and comfortable terrain. Arrival in Sigüeiro.

24 km 5–6 h Difficulty Medium
6

Sigüeiro → Santiago de Compostela

Straightforward final stretch, gradually becoming more urban as you approach. Excitement builds toward arrival at Plaza del Obradoiro in Santiago.

16 km 4 h Difficulty Low
