Route description

Coastal Portuguese Way from Porto

The Coastal Portuguese Way from Porto is one of the most stunning coastal experiences among all Jacobean paths. It starts in the historic Douro city and heads north following beaches and wooden walkways along the Portuguese Atlantic shore.

Throughout the stages, pilgrims pass through coastal towns rich in seafaring tradition such as Vila do Conde, Esposende, Viana do Castelo, and Caminha, where the iconic ferry crossing takes you across the Minho River into Galicia (A Guarda).

In Galicia, the trail follows the ocean through Oia and Baiona, climbs along trails overlooking the Vigo estuary, and merges in Redondela with the central way towards the Obradoiro Square in Santiago.

Highlights

  • Departure from Porto following the Douro River to the Atlantic
  • Wooden boardwalks, sand dunes, and lighthouses of the Portuguese coast
  • Mount Santa Luzia in Viana do Castelo with panoramic ocean vistas
  • Scenic ferry crossing of the Minho River to A Guarda
  • Monastery of Santa María de Oia facing the ocean waves
  • Monterreal Fortress and historic harbour in Baiona
  • Stunning views of the Vigo estuary and viaducts of Redondela
  • Grand arrival at the Cathedral of Santiago de Compostela

Day by day planning

Recommended Stages

1

Porto → Labruge / Vila do Conde

Departure from Porto following the Douro River to the Atlantic. First contact with the sea, beaches, and wooden boardwalks.

22–27 km 5–6 h Difficulty Medium
2

Vila do Conde → Esposende

Very coastal stage with dunes, lighthouses, and fishing villages. Very flat and pleasant.

20–24 km 5–6 h Difficulty Low
3

Esposende → Viana do Castelo

One of the most beautiful sections. Arrival in Viana do Castelo with the option to climb Mount Santa Luzia (spectacular views).

22 km 5h30 Difficulty Medium
4

Viana do Castelo → Caminha

Long but gorgeous stage by the sea. Finishes in Caminha at the mouth of the Minho River.

25 km 6 h Difficulty Medium
5

Caminha → A Guarda (ferry)

Cross the Minho River by boat to A Guarda. Short stage, ideal for resting or exploring the Santa Trega Celtic hill fort.

5 km walking + ferry 2–3 h Difficulty Low
6

A Guarda → Oia

Spectacular stretch right next to the sea. You pass the Monastery of Santa María de Oia, literally facing the ocean.

13–15 km 3–4 h Difficulty Low
7

Oia → Baiona

Follow the coast with continuous views of the Atlantic. Arrival in lively, historic Baiona.

18 km 4h30 Difficulty Medium
8

Baiona → Vigo

Varied stage featuring coast, woodland trails, and urban sections. Arrival in Vigo.

25 km 6 h Difficulty Medium
9

Vigo → Redondela

Ups and downs with views of the estuary. In Redondela you join the inland Portuguese Way.

15–16 km 4 h Difficulty Low
10

Redondela → Pontevedra

Forests, ancient stone paths, and a great pilgrim atmosphere. Arrival in Pontevedra.

19–20 km 4h30 Difficulty Low
11

Pontevedra → Caldas de Reis

Comfortable and green stage. End in Caldas de Reis, famous for its thermal waters.

21 km 5–6 h Difficulty Low
12

Caldas de Reis → Padrón

Relaxed stretch through villages and woods. Arrival in Padrón.

18–19 km 4h30 Difficulty Low
13

Padrón → Santiago de Compostela

Final stage to Santiago de Compostela, culminating at the Cathedral.

24 km 5–6 h Difficulty Medium
