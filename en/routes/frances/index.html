Route description

French Way

The French Way is the Jacobean route with the greatest historical tradition and the most internationally recognized.

It crosses the north of the Iberian Peninsula from Roncesvalles to Santiago de Compostela.

It is a transformative experience full of culture, art, and nature.

Highlights

  • Crossing the Pyrenees from France
  • The Cathedral of Burgos and León
  • The Iron Cross in Astorga
  • The mystical arrival in O Cebreiro
  • Triumphant entry into the Plaza del Obradoiro
