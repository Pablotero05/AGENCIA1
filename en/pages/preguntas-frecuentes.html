Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, straightforward answers compiled by our Galician team to help you plan your pilgrimage with complete confidence.

The Compostela & Pilgrim Passport

Official rules from the Cathedral of Santiago

How many kilometers must I walk to earn the official Compostela certificate?
The Cathedral of Santiago requires walking at least the last 100 kilometers on foot or horseback (or 200 km cycling). Our standard 1-week routes easily qualify:
  • French Way from Sarria: 115 km
  • Portuguese Way from Tui: 117 km
  • English Way from Ferrol: 120 km
  • Primitive Way from Lugo: 100 km
How many stamps do I need each day on my credential?
Over the final 100 km, it is mandatory to obtain at least 2 stamps per day with dates. Stamps can be collected at bars, churches, albergues, hotels, and chapels along the way.
What is the difference between the Credential and the Compostela?
The Credential is your pilgrim passport booklet that you carry and stamp in every town. The Compostela is the official Latin certificate issued by the Pilgrim's Office in Santiago upon verifying your stamps.

Luggage Transfers

Door-to-door daily transfer service

When do I drop off my bag and when does it arrive at the next hotel?
You leave your bag at the hotel reception before 8:00 AM with your Step to Santiago luggage tag. Our drivers deliver it to your next accommodation guaranteed before 2:30 PM.
Is there a weight limit for transferred luggage?
The standard limit is 1 piece of up to 20 kg per person (rolling suitcase or large duffle). Additional bags can easily be added upon request.

Hotels & Included Services

Privacy, comfort, and guaranteed quality

Do all rooms have private en-suite bathrooms?
Yes, 100% of the nights. All properties booked by Step to Santiago are hotels, country manors, or charming guest houses with private en-suite bathrooms inside your room.
Is breakfast included on every stage?
Yes, all our packages include daily breakfast (buffet or continental) at your accommodation or its attached café so you start each walking day fully energized.
What if I have dietary restrictions or am vegetarian/vegan/gluten-free?
Let us know when requesting your quote. We notify all accommodations to adapt your breakfasts (gluten-free bread, plant-based milk, etc.) and recommend suitable restaurants in each town.
What exactly does a Step to Santiago package include?
It includes private room accommodation with en-suite bathrooms, daily breakfast, daily door-to-door luggage transfers (up to 20 kg per person), official pilgrim credentials, detailed route guidebook with stage maps, and 24/7 local telephone assistance.
How are airport and train transfers arranged?
We organize private door-to-door transfers from major airports and stations (Santiago, A Coruña, Vigo, Porto, Madrid) directly to your starting hotel, as well as return transfers after finishing in Santiago.

Fitness & Emergency Support

Preparation, footwear, and on-trail care

Do I need to be an athlete to walk the Camino?
Extreme athletic fitness is not required, but a habit of regular walking is essential. We recommend walking 8-12 km a few times per week during the month before departure, using the exact footwear and socks you plan to bring.
What type of footwear is recommended?
Breathable trail running shoes or lightweight hiking boots with good cushioning and grippy soles. Golden rule: never wear brand-new shoes on the Camino; break them in for at least 3 weeks beforehand.
What happens if I get tired midway and cannot continue walking?
You have our 24/7 local telephone assistance. Your travel dossier includes verified local taxi contacts for each stage to pick you up and take you directly to your hotel.
