Section 1

What are cookies and similar technologies?

A cookie is a small text file or identifier that websites store on your computer, smartphone, or tablet when you visit specific pages.

Cookies and related storage technologies (such as localStorage or sessionStorage) enable websites to remember your browsing preferences (such as preferred language or privacy consent states), facilitating subsequent visits and tailoring your experience.

Section 2

Cookie Categories & Purposes

Technical & Strictly Necessary Cookies

Essential for basic website operations, security verification, session management, and recording your privacy consent decisions. These do not require prior consent under European legislation.

Analytics & Performance Cookies

Advertising & Marketing Cookies

Track visitor behavior across websites to build a profile for serving relevant advertisements and evaluating marketing campaigns. Subject to explicit prior consent.

Functional & Personalization Cookies

Remember user-defined options such as interface language and regional settings to provide an enhanced, personalized browsing experience.
