Sección 1

Definitions

A. Package travel

Package travel is understood as that service which integrates two or more tourist services (such as transport, accommodation or other related services) within the same contract and for the same travel experience.

B. Travel service

Any service included in the itinerary, such as the transport of passengers, accommodation, vehicle rental or other complementary tourist services.

C. Travel contract

Formal agreement through which one or more contracted travel services are collected, including all applicable conditions to the package travel.

D. Start date

Day on which the services included in the contracted trip begin to be executed.

E. Repatriation

Process by which the traveler is transferred back to their place of origin or another destination previously agreed upon between the parties.

F. Lack of conformity

Situation in which the services provided are not executed correctly or do not conform to what was agreed in the contract.

G. Traveler

Person who contracts or has the right to benefit from a trip under the tourist services contract.

H. Tourist company

Entity responsible for the organization, marketing or provision of the travel services included in the contracted program.

I. Trip organizer

Company that combines the different tourist services and offers them as a joint trip, either directly or through third parties.

J. Retail agency

Entity that markets or sells package tours organized by a third party, without being the organizing company.

L. Minor

Any person who has not reached the age of 18 at the time of contracting or travel.

M. Extraordinary circumstances

Exceptional and unforeseeable situations, beyond the control of the organization, whose consequences could not have been avoided even by adopting all reasonable measures.

Sección 2

Important notes on package travel

2.1 Organization of the package travel

The planning and management of the package travel correspond to TU BUEN CAMINO, as the entity responsible for the coordination of all the services included.

2.2 Package travel contract

Once the reservation is confirmed, the traveler will receive a copy of the package travel contract within a maximum period of 24 hours from its formalization. This contract will include, as an essential part, the general conditions, the prior information provided to the traveler and the specific conditions agreed upon in each reservation.

2.3 Price, reservation and payment method

The price of the package travel is established based on the contracted services and will be reflected in the reservation confirmation. To guarantee the spot, it will be necessary to make the payment according to the conditions indicated at the time of contracting.

The price INCLUDES

  • All tourist services detailed in the travel documentation
  • Management expenses, commissions and charges associated with the reservation
  • Traveler assistance in case of incidents during the tour
  • Support in the processing of complaints or claims related to the service

The price DOES NOT INCLUDE

  • Necessary personal documentation, such as visas or health certificates
  • Additional unspecified consumptions (drinks, snacks or special diets)
  • Optional services such as spa, massages or other treatments
  • Local tourist taxes, which must be paid directly at the accommodation
  • Taxes or fees applicable in airports or other transport points, unless expressly indicated in the program

Reservation process

  1. Reservation request: Fill out and send the reservation form through our website with the necessary data to organize your trip.
  2. Payment of the trip: Make the corresponding payment to guarantee your spot, according to the conditions indicated in the contracting process.
  3. Reservation confirmation: The reservation will only be confirmed when you receive our confirmation email with all the details.
  4. Travel documentation: A few days before the start of the Camino you will receive all the necessary information, including contracted services, accommodations and recommendations for your route.

Reservations with less than 7 days in advance

For reservations made less than 7 days before the start of the trip, full payment by credit card will be necessary before 12:00 h on the same day the confirmation is requested. In case it is not possible to confirm the services by the agency, the full amount paid will be refunded within a maximum period of 12 hours.

2.4 Assistance

The traveler may address the organizer at any time to send queries, requests or claims related to their trip. In case of unavoidable or extraordinary situations, appropriate assistance will be provided as quickly as possible, in order to minimize any incident during the tour. If due to these circumstances it is not possible to guarantee the return on the scheduled date, accommodation expenses will be covered for a maximum of three nights, under the conditions established by applicable regulations.

2.5 Modification of the contract

The organization may introduce changes to the contract before the start of the trip, provided these are minor and communicated to the traveler with due advance notice.

Modifications exceeding 8%

In case the price increase exceeds 8% or there are substantial changes in the main services of the trip, the traveler will have the right to terminate the contract without any penalty.

2.6 Transfer of the contract

The traveler may transfer their spot to another person provided they meet the necessary requirements for the trip. This transfer must be communicated to the organization with a minimum of 7 calendar days before the start of the service. Both the original traveler and the person substituting them will be jointly responsible for the pending payment and any additional expense derived from the transfer.

2.7 Payments and refunds

To enjoy all the services included in the trip, it must be fully paid before its start. If the agency proceeds to terminate the contract, the corresponding amount will be refunded to the traveler within a maximum period of 14 calendar days from the notification. If the traveler does not appear on the agreed date and time for the start of the services, they will have no right to any refund. Likewise, the premium corresponding to the cancellation insurance, if contracted, will not be refundable under any circumstances.

2.8 Termination of the contract by the traveler

Penalty for cancellation

The cancellation of the trip by the traveler will imply a penalty of 100% of the total amount when made within 15 days prior to the start of the trip. In the case of groups of 12 or more people, this period is extended to 30 days before the start of the trip.

Right to terminate without penalty and full refund

The traveler may terminate the contract without any cost and with the right to a full refund of the amounts paid in the following cases:

  • When unavoidable and extraordinary circumstances occur at the destination that significantly affect the execution of the trip.
  • If there is a substantial modification of essential elements of the contract, excluding the price.
  • In case of cancellation of the trip by the agency before its start.
  • When there is a relevant lack of execution of the contracted services.
  • In exceptional situations that affect the safety of the traveler or the development of the trip.

Sección 3

General considerations

3.1 Documentation

Each traveler is responsible for having valid documentation necessary to carry out the trip, including ID, passport, visas and any health requirement that may be demanded. If the trip is cancelled due to the lack or incorrect validity of such documentation, the established cancellation conditions will apply.

3.2 Luggage transfer service

The luggage transport service is only provided in stages done on foot or by bicycle. Transfer in means of transport such as bus, train or motorized vehicle is excluded.

Important: The organization reserves the right to suspend the service to any user who causes incidents or disturbances during its provision. Likewise, no responsibility is assumed for loss, damage or deterioration of luggage, except in legally established cases.

3.3 Category of accommodations

The accommodations included in the trips may correspond to different categories, including:

  • 1 to 5 star hotels
  • 1 to 3 star pensions
  • Rural houses
  • 1 to 4 key tourist apartments
  • Tourist hostels
  • Housing for tourist use

3.4 Insurance

The agency will offer information to the traveler about the contracting of optional insurance that may include coverage such as:

  • Cancellation expenses due to unforeseen causes or force majeure
  • Assistance in case of accident during the trip
  • Repatriation in situations of serious illness or death
